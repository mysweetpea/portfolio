Why MySweetPea?

Privacy, control, and community — without the corporate overhead

Privacy First

Your data stays on our server, not on some corporate cloud. No tracking, no ads, no data selling. Everything you do here is private.

Self-Hosted

All services run on hardware we own, not rented from big tech companies. This means full control and no middleman.

Community-Funded

No recurring subscriptions or ads. Community contributions help protect limited capacity and keep the infrastructure running.

Open Source

Every service we use is free and open source. You can verify exactly how everything works on our public GitHub.

Crypto Donations

We accept Monero and Bitcoin. No personal info required, no credit checks, no paper trail. Your donation is anonymous.

Community Driven

Want a new service? Suggest it and we will consider adding it. This project is shaped by the community.
