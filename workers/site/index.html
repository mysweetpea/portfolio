00 Before anything else

            
What MySweetPea actually is.

            

                

                    
MySweetPea is a gateway to a more private life. It is your private garden — a server built from the ground up to host multiple free and open-source apps for you to use.

                    
You create a MySweetPea account and use it to sign in to any of the available services without having to set anything up yourself. Photos back up like Google Photos, movies stream like Netflix, passwords sync like 1Password. However, all of the services you can use aren't funded by tracking you, selling your data, or any type of ads or subscriptions.

                    
MySweetPea is donation based, and to get access is simply a one-time donation.

                

                

                    
In plain terms

                    
MySweetPea runs on self-hosted, open-source services. This means all of the services offered by MySweetPea run on my own hardware (self-hosting) instead of rented hardware from a big cloud company that would track your usage. It also means each service is auditable (open-source): you can view whether the service you're using tracks you or builds a profile on you — unlike proprietary services, where you can't see what they're up to.

07 Questions

            
Asked & answered.

            

                
Why is access invite-only?+
Capacity is real — hardware, bandwidth and one gardener. Invites keep the garden fast and supported for the people already in it. When capacity grows, access opens.

                
What happens to my data if this disappears?+
Every service exports in standard formats — files, calendars, photos, passwords. No lock-in is a design rule here, not a promise.

                
Could I just run all this myself?+
Yes — and that’s the point. This entire garden ships as a free, open-source installer: a guided wizard, 54 apps, any Linux box, no GitHub account required. MySweetPea is convenience, not captivity — use it hosted, take it home, or move between the two with standard-format exports.

                
Is it really free of tracking?+
No analytics, no trackers, no ad networks. The only public metrics are the uptime monitors on the status page.

                
Can I request a new service?+
Yes — that is the roadmap. Members suggest, the garden grows. Use the Suggest a Service link in the footer.

                
What does it cost?+
A one-time community contribution — amount confirmed in your invite email. It covers power, hardware, bandwidth and the domain. No subscription, no renewal, ever.
