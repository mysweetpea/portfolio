How It Works

MySweetPea runs private, self-hosted services on our own hardware. Access is managed through Authentik, our single sign-on service.

Create one Authentik account, then use it to sign in to every service in your tier. No separate account is needed for each service.

Choose a tier below to request access. Once your contribution is verified, you receive a redemption code to create your Authentik account and get started.

Why a $5 minimum contribution?
A small community contribution helps prevent spam and abuse, ensures members are invested in the community, and contributes to infrastructure costs such as electricity, hardware, and bandwidth. It is not a subscription or a sale of your data.
