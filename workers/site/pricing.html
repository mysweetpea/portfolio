Why a donation instead of a subscription? ▼

Subscriptions create ongoing billing relationships that require storing your payment info. A one-time crypto donation is simpler, more private, and covers your lifetime access. It also keeps us accountable to the community rather than to recurring revenue.

Can I upgrade from Seedling to Sweet Pea later? ▼

Yes. Just make another $5 donation with the same email and your account will be upgraded to Sweet Pea automatically.

Is my data really private? ▼

Yes. Everything runs on our own hardware, not rented cloud servers. Services like Vaultwarden use end-to-end encryption, meaning even we cannot see your data. We do not run ads, sell data, or track you.

What happens if the project shuts down? ▼

All the software we run is open source, so you can export your data and self-host it yourself. There is no vendor lock-in — your passwords, files, and photos are always exportable in standard formats.
