How It Works

Getting access takes three simple steps. No technical knowledge needed.

1

Choose a tier

Pick Seedling or Sweet Pea below — whichever services you want to use.

2

Fill out the tier form

Tell us which tier you want. Seedling includes a $5 crypto donation; Sweet Pea is reviewed after you submit your request.

3

Create your account

We email you an invite code. Use it to set up your one MySweetPea sign-in — the same username and password works for every service.

Your sign-in page is powered by software called Authentik — that is just its name, not a typo. Stuck at any point? Email support@mysweetpea.cc and we'll walk you through it.

Why a $5 minimum contribution?
A small community contribution helps prevent spam and abuse, ensures members are invested in the community, and contributes to infrastructure costs such as electricity, hardware, and bandwidth. It is not a subscription or a sale of your data.
