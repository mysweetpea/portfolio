Navigation bar — three directions

Each drawn from one of your reference sites, rebuilt with MySweetPea's real tokens (nordic teal, sage, ice, Fraunces + Inter). The mock page behind each bar is there so transparency, blur and borders read truthfully.

1

Borderless

inspired by omarchy

No bar at all. Links sit directly on the page with nothing behind them — the most minimal of the three, and the closest to "private garden" (nothing encloses it). A hairline background only appears once you scroll, so it stays legible over content. The button is the one solid object: ice-white on dark, echoing omarchy's cream-on-black.

Crowned

inspired by hermes

The wordmark moves to the dead centre and the links split evenly around it — a symmetrical, ceremonial layout that makes the name itself the focal point. Set in Fraunces, your serif, it reads more like a masthead than a menu. Links go small, uppercase and widely tracked, as in hermes. This is the most distinctive of the three and the one that leans hardest into your editorial identity.
