Changelog

What's new at MySweetPea — service updates, improvements, and community changes.

August 2026

  • New services live: Nextcloud (files & calendar), Immich (photo backup), and Open WebUI (private AI chat) are now available to Sweet Pea members.
  • Reliability: Automated database and configuration backups are now running daily. Namespace protection added to prevent accidental data loss.
  • Performance: All services updated to their latest stable versions, including n8n 2.33.3 and Authentik 2026.5.6.

July 2026

  • Website redesign: New Nordic Frost theme, falling-petal animations, glassmorphism, and a command palette (Ctrl+K).
  • Community access: Introduced the Seedling and Sweet Pea tiers with a one-time $5 minimum donation.
  • New service: KoalaSync (watch parties) added behind single sign-on.

June 2026

  • Single sign-on: All services now use one MySweetPea sign-in via Authentik.
  • New services: Jellyfin / Moonfin (media), Seerr (requests), and AFFiNE (notes & docs) launched.
  • Infrastructure: Migrated to GitOps with ArgoCD for reproducible deployments.
